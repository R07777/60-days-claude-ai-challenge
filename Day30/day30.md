# Day 30 – Supply Chain Builder

## Overview
Today I explored how supply chain decisions impact business performance by building an interactive Supply Chain Builder simulator using React.

## What I Did
- Generated a single-file React HTML application.
- Explored a randomly generated company profile.
- Selected supplier strategy, factory location, warehouse strategy, transportation method, and inventory strategy.
- Observed live business metrics such as Cost, Delivery Speed, Risk, Customer Satisfaction, and Sustainability.
- Reviewed the final optimization dashboard with Overall Supply Chain Score, strengths, weaknesses, risks, and recommendations.

## Key Learnings
- Every supply chain decision involves trade-offs.
- Multiple suppliers reduce risk but may increase costs.
- Transportation choices directly affect delivery speed and sustainability.
- Inventory strategies balance customer satisfaction and operational expenses.
- Data-driven decisions lead to a more resilient supply chain.
