# Day 23 – Customer & MVP Blueprint

## Objective

Created a Customer & MVP Blueprint based on the Startup Validation Report generated in Day 22.

## Activities Performed

* Reviewed the Startup Validation Report.
* Extracted startup idea, target customers, and validation insights.
* Generated the Customer & MVP Blueprint.
* Analyzed:

  * Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
  * Buyer Persona
  * Customer Pain Points
  * Customer Journey
  * Buying Triggers & Objections
  * MVP Recommendation
  * MoSCoW Prioritization
  * Pricing Hypothesis
  * Top Risks
  * Founder Action Sheet
* Saved the generated report and screenshots.

## Key Learnings

* Understanding customers is more important than building extra features.
* MVP should focus only on solving the core problem.
* Customer interviews help validate assumptions early.
* Prioritization frameworks like MoSCoW reduce development waste.
* A clear ICP improves product-market fit chances.

## Outcome

Successfully generated and reviewed the Customer & MVP Blueprint report, identified customer needs, prioritized MVP features, and documented startup action items.
