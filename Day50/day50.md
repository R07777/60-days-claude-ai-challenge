## 🚀 Project

## 📖 Overview

Today I built **Defend Your Experience**, an AI-powered interview preparation platform that helps users confidently defend every claim made in their resume, portfolio, LinkedIn profile, or project documentation.

The application extracts meaningful claims, generates adaptive follow-up interview questions, identifies weak evidence, and produces a comprehensive Defense Report.
