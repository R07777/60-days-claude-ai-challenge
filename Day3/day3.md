# Day 3 - Role-Based Prompting

## Objective

Understand how assigning different roles changes AI responses.

## Question Used

How can I improve my career as a Computer Science Engineering student and get better internship opportunities?

## Response 1 - Without Role

### Observation

The response was balanced and general. It covered:

* DSA and technical skills
* Projects
* Internships
* Networking
* Communication skills

The advice was useful but broad.

## Response 2 - Founder Role

### Observation

The response focused on:

* Building real products
* Startup mindset
* Networking
* Personal branding
* Writing publicly
* Creating opportunities instead of waiting for them

The advice was more entrepreneurial and growth-oriented.

## Response 3 - Developer Role

### Observation

The response focused on:

* DSA practice
* CS fundamentals
* GitHub projects
* Open source contributions
* Competitive programming
* Technical interview preparation

The advice was more technical and career-focused.

## Key Learnings

1. AI changes its perspective based on the assigned role.
2. Founder personas provide business and growth-oriented advice.
3. Developer personas provide technical and implementation-focused advice.
4. Role-Based Prompting produces more specialized and useful responses.

## Conclusion

Role-Based Prompting helps transform AI into a domain-specific expert. By assigning a role, users can receive more relevant, focused, and actionable guidance.
