Day 16 – Custom Skill: Stock Fundamental Research

Objective

Created a reusable Claude Custom Skill named stock-fundamental-research to analyze listed companies using fundamental analysis, valuation metrics, financial performance, ownership trends, business quality, growth prospects, and risk factors.

Activities Performed

- Created a new Claude Custom Skill.
- Added the provided description and instructions.
- Saved the skill successfully.
- Tested the skill using Tata Consultancy Services (TCS).
- Generated a stock research report.
- Reviewed valuation metrics, growth indicators, ownership trends, and risk factors.
- Observed how the skill can be reused without entering the full prompt again.

Key Learnings

- Custom Skills help automate repetitive workflows.
- Fundamental analysis focuses on business quality rather than short-term price movements.
- Reusable AI skills improve productivity and consistency.
- Financial data should always be verified from trusted sources before making decisions.

Outcome

Successfully created and tested a reusable stock research skill in Claude and generated a sample stock analysis report.
