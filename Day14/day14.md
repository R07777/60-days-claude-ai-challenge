Day 14 – Job Red Flag Detector

Objective

Analyze a Job Description and Company Information to identify potential risks, red flags, and positive signals before applying for a job.

Tasks Completed

- Collected Job Description and Company Information.
- Used Claude AI to perform Job Red Flag Analysis.
- Reviewed Overall Risk Score.
- Analyzed Red Flags and Positive Signals.
- Examined Risk Breakdown across multiple categories.
- Reviewed Final Verdict and Interview Questions.
- Saved the generated report and screenshots.

Key Findings

- Identified potential hiring risks and unrealistic requirements.
- Evaluated workplace culture indicators.
- Analyzed company stability and reputation factors.
- Reviewed job authenticity and remote work claims.
- Generated smart interview questions for validation.

Learnings

- Not every job posting is equally trustworthy.
- Reviewing red flags helps avoid risky opportunities.
- Company research is important before applying.
- Asking the right interview questions can reveal hidden concerns.
- AI tools can assist in making informed career decisions.

Outcome

Successfully completed Day 14 of the 60 Days Claude AI Challenge and learned how to evaluate job opportunities more effectively using AI-powered analysis.
