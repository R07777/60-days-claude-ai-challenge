# Day 13 – AI-Powered Job Discovery & Market Analysis

## Objective

Use AI-assisted job discovery tools to identify relevant opportunities, analyze market demand, evaluate skill gaps, and understand hiring trends for target roles.

## Activities Performed

* Created a professional profile highlighting skills, projects, certifications, and career goals.
* Defined job search preferences including role type, work arrangement, salary expectations, and company preferences.
* Used AI-powered job discovery and analysis workflows.
* Explored matching opportunities based on profile and preferences.
* Reviewed skill-gap recommendations and market demand insights.
* Studied recruiter expectations and industry trends.

## Deliverables

* Generated job discovery and analysis results.
* AI-generated opportunity matching insights.
* Market demand and skill-gap analysis.
* Screenshot of the generated application/dashboard.

## Key Learnings

* Learned how AI can assist in identifying suitable job opportunities.
* Understood the importance of aligning skills with market requirements.
* Gained insights into recruiter expectations and hiring trends.
* Improved understanding of career planning and job search strategies.
* Learned how data-driven analysis can support better career decisions.

## Outcome

Successfully completed Day 13 by exploring AI-assisted job discovery, analyzing opportunities, reviewing skill gaps, and documenting key career insights.
