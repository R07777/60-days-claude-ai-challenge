Recommendations to improve interview chances

01

Deploy 2–3 live MERN projects with real URLs

A deployed e-commerce app, task manager, or dashboard on Vercel/Render beats a private GitHub repo in every recruiter conversation.

02

Add TypeScript to at least one project

Rewrite a JS project in TS over a weekend — it signals seriousness and readiness for production codebases.

03

Optimize your LinkedIn headline and apply directly through it

"Full Stack Developer | MERN | React | Node.js | Open to Work" — keyword-driven headlines get recruiter inbound messages.

04

Apply on Naukri.com, Internshala, and Indeed simultaneously

63+ fresher full stack roles are live on Internshala alone this month. Spread applications across all three platforms for maximum reach.

05

Prepare for DSA + system design basics

Even junior-level interviews at product companies and funded startups include LeetCode-easy/medium problems and basic architecture questions.
