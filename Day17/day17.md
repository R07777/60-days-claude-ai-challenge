Day 17 - Vehicle Cost Analysis Dashboard

Objective

Built an AI-powered Vehicle Cost Analysis Dashboard using Claude and a CSV dataset to analyze fuel costs, maintenance expenses, environmental impact, and vehicle ownership economics.

Tasks Completed

- Downloaded and reviewed the provided CSV dataset.
- Configured vehicle details and dashboard parameters.
- Uploaded dataset to Claude.
- Generated a complete HTML dashboard.
- Reviewed KPI cards, fuel comparisons, maintenance analysis, and environmental impact metrics.
- Analyzed E85 economics and break-even calculations.
- Explored interactive charts, gauges, and fuel comparison insights.
- Saved the generated HTML dashboard and tested it in a browser.

Key Learnings

- Learned how AI can transform raw CSV data into interactive dashboards.
- Understood cost-per-kilometer and maintenance cost analysis.
- Explored fuel efficiency and environmental impact comparisons.
- Gained experience with HTML-based data visualization dashboards.
- Learned how data-driven insights support better decision-making.

Outcome

Successfully generated and reviewed a Vehicle Cost Analysis Dashboard using Claude AI and explored vehicle operating costs through data visualization.
