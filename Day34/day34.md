# Day 34 – Marketing Detective

## Overview
Today I built and explored **Marketing Detective**, an interactive detective-style learning application that teaches marketing strategy through fictional campaign investigations. Instead of simply reading marketing concepts, users investigate campaign failures, analyze evidence, identify mistakes, and learn the reasoning behind successful marketing decisions.

## What I Did
- Generated a single-file HTML application.
- Selected a preferred color theme.
- Accepted a randomized marketing investigation.
- Explored the investigation board.
- Organized and analyzed evidence cards.
- Identified the primary marketing mistake.
- Reviewed the expert explanation and final verdict.
- Studied the Marketing Learning Report.
- Replayed multiple randomized marketing cases.

## Key Learnings
- Marketing success depends on understanding the target audience.
- Budget allocation should align with campaign objectives.
- Campaign metrics reveal the real performance of marketing efforts.
- Customer feedback provides valuable insights for future improvements.
- Evidence-based analysis leads to better marketing decisions.
