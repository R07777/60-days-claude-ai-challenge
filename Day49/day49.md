## 📖 Overview

Today I built **Personal AI Playbook**, a personalized AI workflow management platform that helps organize reusable prompt systems, modular AI workflows, and autonomous prompt loops based on individual productivity needs.

Instead of storing hundreds of static prompts, the application focuses on building reusable AI systems that can generate thousands of workflow variations through modular building blocks.
