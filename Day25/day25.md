# Day 25 – AI Shark Tank Simulator

## Overview

Today I built an **AI Shark Tank Simulator** using Claude AI. The application simulates a startup pitch environment where users can enter their startup idea and receive feedback from multiple AI investor personas.

## Features Implemented

* Startup Idea Input Form
* Venture Capitalist Judge
* Founder Judge
* Customer Judge
* Angel Investor Judge
* Interactive Pitch Round
* AI-generated Questions & Responses
* Startup Scorecard
* Investment Decision Engine
* Suggested Valuation & Funding
* Leaderboard System
* Downloadable Pitch Report
* Share Results Feature
* Responsive Shark Tank Style UI

## What I Learned

* How AI can simulate investor thinking.
* Importance of market validation and scalability.
* Startup evaluation metrics such as:

  * Market Potential
  * Innovation
  * Business Model
  * Execution Capability
  * Investment Worthiness
* Creating engaging interactive HTML applications with AI.

## Challenges Faced

* Handling multiple investor perspectives.
* Designing scoring logic.
* Building a professional Shark Tank style interface.

## Outcome

Successfully generated and tested the AI Shark Tank Simulator. The application provided startup analysis, investor feedback, valuation estimates, and funding recommendations in an interactive format.

## Key Takeaway

AI can be used as a virtual startup mentor and investor panel to quickly evaluate business ideas before presenting them to real investors.
