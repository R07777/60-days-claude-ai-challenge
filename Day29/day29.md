# Day 29 – Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab

## Objective

Built and explored an interactive Supply Chain Crisis simulation using Claude AI. The application generates random business scenarios, crisis events, supplier negotiations, leadership decisions, AI strategy selections, and a final executive performance dashboard.

## What I Completed

* Generated the complete single-file React HTML application.
* Tested the simulation with multiple random company profiles.
* Reviewed supply chain crisis scenarios and selected response strategies.
* Completed supplier negotiation rounds.
* Answered CEO leadership questions.
* Selected AI investments for business resilience.
* Explored the final executive dashboard and business performance scores.
* Captured screenshots of the application and simulation results.

## Key Learnings

* Supply chain resilience requires quick and balanced decision-making.
* Leadership choices directly impact business outcomes.
* AI can improve forecasting, inventory management, and supplier risk monitoring.
* Every crisis requires evaluating trade-offs between cost, customer satisfaction, and operational continuity.

## Outcome

Successfully completed the Operation Lifeline Supply Chain Crisis Lab and understood how AI-assisted simulations can support supply chain and business strategy learning.
