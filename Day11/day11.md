# Day 11 - ATS Resume Optimizer & Resume Generator

## Objective

Optimize an existing resume using ATS (Applicant Tracking System) principles and align it with a target Job Description.

## Tasks Completed

* Uploaded existing resume to Claude.
* Selected a relevant Full Stack Developer / Software Developer Job Description.
* Generated ATS Match Score.
* Reviewed Gap Analysis and missing keywords.
* Generated ATS-optimized resume.
* Saved optimized resume.
* Captured screenshots of ATS analysis and optimized resume.
* Uploaded all deliverables to GitHub.

## ATS Analysis

The ATS analysis highlighted important keywords, skills, and improvements required to better align the resume with the target job description.

## Learnings

* Learned how ATS systems scan resumes.
* Understood the importance of job-specific keywords.
* Improved resume structure and readability.
* Learned how to optimize resumes without adding false information.
* Gained knowledge about recruiter-friendly resume formatting.
