Nutritional Data Sources

NutriScope uses nutritional values derived from the following authoritative databases and references. All values are per 100g unless otherwise stated and represent commonly consumed forms of each food.

🇮🇳 ICMR — Nutritive Value of Indian Foods (NIN)
🌐 USDA FoodData Central
🌍 FAO / WHO Food Composition Tables
📋 Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT 2017)
📖 Dietary Reference Intakes — IOM
🩺 WHO Nutrient Reference Values
Calculation Methods
BMR: Mifflin–St Jeor Equation (1990)
TDEE: BMR × Activity Factor (Harris–Benedict categories)
Protein: 0.8–1.2g × body weight (ICMR RDA)
Carbs: 50–60% of total energy
Fat: 20–35% of total energy
Micronutrients: ICMR RDA 2020 for Indian adults
BMI: WHO classification (Asian cut-offs)
