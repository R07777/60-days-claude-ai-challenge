# Day 21 - Digital Privacy Dashboard

## Overview

Built an interactive Digital Privacy Dashboard to analyze a user's digital footprint, privacy exposure, tracking surface, and data collection risks using a sample dataset.

## Features Explored

* Digital Footprint Score
* Privacy Score Analysis
* Exposure Heatmap
* Company Exposure Ranking
* Data Collection Matrix
* Risk Radar Visualization
* Digital Twin Profile
* Most Valuable Data Assets
* Privacy Improvement Simulator
* Privacy Recommendations & Final Verdict

## Dataset Used

Applications:

* Instagram
* Snapchat
* TikTok
* YouTube
* Discord
* WhatsApp
* iMessage
* Spotify
* Roblox
* PUBG Mobile
* Amazon
* Meesho
* Google Search
* Google Pay
* Google Photos

## Key Findings

* Multiple services belong to a small number of parent companies.
* High ecosystem concentration increases potential tracking exposure.
* Communication, entertainment, shopping, and payment platforms contribute significantly to the digital footprint.
* Privacy posture can be improved by reducing unnecessary permissions and enabling stronger security controls.

## Key Learnings

* Learned how digital footprints are created through everyday online activities.
* Understood ecosystem concentration and cross-platform tracking risks.
* Explored privacy scoring methodologies and exposure analysis.
* Learned how dashboards can visualize cybersecurity and privacy insights.

## Technologies Used

* HTML5
* CSS3
* JavaScript
* Interactive Dashboard Design
* Data Visualization Concepts

## Outcome

Successfully created and reviewed a Digital Privacy Dashboard highlighting digital exposure, privacy risks, and actionable improvement strategies.
