The Google Trap

The TikTok X-Factor
TikTok's parent ByteDance operates under a different legal framework than Western apps. Your device fingerprint, keystroke patterns, and behavioural data may be subject to non-EU/Indian legal protections. Risk is structural, not just technical.

Privacy Improvement Plan
1
Audit Google Account Data & Activity Controls
Visit myaccount.google.com → Data & Privacy. Turn off Web & App Activity auto-save, Location History, and YouTube watch history. Delete existing data buckets. This is the single highest-ROI privacy action given 4 active Google services.
Impact: Very High
2
Limit TikTok Permissions Aggressively
Revoke location, microphone, clipboard access in phone settings. Consider using TikTok only via browser (web.tiktok.com) to reduce device-level fingerprinting. Evaluate whether continued use is acceptable given jurisdictional data risk.
Impact: High
3
Enable Instagram & Facebook Off-Platform Activity Controls
Meta's "Off-Facebook Activity" tool lets you see and disconnect third-party data that Meta receives about you from other sites and apps. This directly reduces cross-site ad tracking profile depth.
Impact: High
4
Use Separate Browsers for Shopping vs Social
Amazon + Meesho in a dedicated browser (e.g. Brave) prevents cross-contamination with Google Search intent data. This breaks ad retargeting chains between your purchase intent and social ad feeds.
Impact: Medium
5
Review App Permissions on Your Device
For each app, audit: location (set to "While Using" only), microphone, camera, contacts, and clipboard access. PUBG Mobile and Snapchat commonly request broader permissions than their core function requires.
Impact: Medium
6
Enable Disappearing Messages on WhatsApp & Discord
Set all WhatsApp chats to 7-day or 24-hour disappearing messages. Enable auto-delete in Discord DMs. This limits the metadata and content window available to the platform even if data is ever breached or subpoenaed.
Impact: Moderate
7
Replace Google Photos with a Private Alternative
Consider Ente Photos or Proton Drive for photo backup. Both offer end-to-end encryption. Google Photos uses your uploaded faces to train its ML models — switching removes ongoing biometric data contribution.
Impact: Moderate
