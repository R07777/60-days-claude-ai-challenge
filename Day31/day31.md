# Day 31 – AI Supply Chain Control Tower

## Overview
Today I built and explored an interactive **AI Supply Chain Control Tower** simulation. The application replicates the role of a Head of Operations responsible for monitoring supply chain disruptions, prioritizing operational alerts, and making strategic decisions under time pressure.

## What I Did
- Generated a single-file HTML application.
- Monitored real-time operational alerts.
- Prioritized incidents based on urgency and business impact.
- Selected corrective actions for supply chain disruptions.
- Observed live KPI changes after every decision.
- Managed multiple simultaneous alerts.
- Completed the simulation and reviewed the final operational dashboard.

## Key Learnings
- Effective prioritization is essential during supply chain disruptions.
- Every operational decision directly affects customer satisfaction, cost, and service levels.
- Data-driven decision-making improves operational resilience.
- Balancing speed, efficiency, and cost is critical for successful supply chain management.
